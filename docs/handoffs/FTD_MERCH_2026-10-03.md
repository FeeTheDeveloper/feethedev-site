Before activating checkout, confirm the available sizes and colors, fulfillment capacity, shipping charge and destination countries, and whether the supplied mockups match production garments. Review a print-quality proof for the garment art. Then create Stripe Payment Links with product options and shipping address collection, inspect each link's line items and amount, and set the four `MERCH_*_STRIPE_LINK` environment variables in Vercel. The page validates that each link uses HTTPS on `buy.stripe.com`; until then, it offers an inquiry button.

The merch route is local source only until a reviewed commit and Vercel deployment are made. Its current navigation and sitemap entries will make the page public when deployed.
